### Review on 2026-11-27: a window at alpha 0 hides its group (1fec0c6, 72b2a24)

The PS4 rule (0x5A5E0, 0x51060; `scratch/K53/review-traces/B`, `scratch/K53/review-fix`): a window's group (its picture at forced alpha 1, the held layers and the ridge) is drawn into an offscreen list and captured. In list 0 the engine sets the destination alpha to 0 over the screen (effect 55, 10 x 10 canvas), lets only the window's own mask pass write it (0x51060 with esi = 1, effect 58, at min(1, alpha + alpha_cur), or 1 when sttr >= 0) and lays the capture through it (effect 26). 0x51060 draws nothing in that pass at alpha <= 0 (0x510FA..0x5112D). So a group's on-screen weight is its window's alpha, crop rectangle or not, and 7ba52aa's crop / no-crop split had no eboot basis (the port's mask jumped from 0.031 at alpha 0.033 to 1.0 at alpha 0).

- 1fec0c6 removes `maskOnly`: `DrawLayer` is the v0.1.10 code again. 72b2a24 also drops the ridge of a window at alpha 0 with sttr < 0 (it is inside the capture); this removes the v0.1.9 / v0.1.10 seam of window 24's black ridge. Above alpha 0 the ridge is still drawn at full strength over its window, as in v0.1.10, which is exact only over a backdrop of the ridge's colour; scene 320's black seams show at full strength on its panels' first visible frames. Weighting the ridge by the window's alpha (e5b4c7f, reverted in 7ba6cff) removed that but moved four other fading scenes further from the PS4 (`scratch/K53/ridge-check`).
- Static reveal: pure white (every second step 4076..4120), as the engine predicts. Animated reveal: unchanged. Its 15 `_STCF` waits (default mask 0x3F), one sound per slice and `_WTTM(800)` do schedule right / middle / left (STCF(28) 29 frames, STCF(33) 18, WTTM 48), but on records that are not drawn yet; at the single commit (0x8679) windows 28 / 33 / 36 are at alpha 1 with motion frames 109 / 75 / 49 in engine and port, so all three appear at once and fade to white over 48 frames. `_FANX` is a PS4 no-op. No PS4 rule staggers them.
- Scene 302: Mio's first cut-in (window 7, alpha 0) is no longer shown on the two pages before `_STMA(7,152)` (0x3989) and then faded in again; it now fades in once with that motion, as the engine traces it (alpha 0 and no effect-58 pass until frame 185, then 0.055 → 1.0). Her second cut-in (window 16, alpha 0, never animated) is not shown at all; the engine trace has no effect-58 pass for it. Both pictures equal the v0.1.10 captures.
- Corrections to the 2026-11-26 follow-up: "no inter-window wait" holds only for the static block (above). `inspect_tram.py`'s scenarios dropped WTTM / FANX / SNX4 / SEPL, gave STCF no frames and ran four frames per commit; their "draw" list counted the offscreen pass-1 sprite (effect 34, alpha forced to 1), not what reaches the screen, and the 40-state match was at motion frames 0..4.

### Turned windows on 2026-11-27 (68c3407, 1641bac, 8f87461, b0fcaa0, 5337feb)

The PS4 turns a crop window by its angle (section 4), and the owner asked for it. 1641bac draws it for windows with a nonzero angle only:

- The scissor is only the turned crop's bounds. The window's own quads are masked by the turned crop: soft modes fade on all four sides, ridge modes are cut hard at the inner rectangle (as the straight scissor cuts them), and inset 0 is the bare crop.
- Held layers get silhouette × turned mask. Four turned effect-55 bands clear the rest of the mask, the PS4's own way, because a multiply only narrows the mask where it draws. A rule window is narrowed the same way (no retail script turns one).
- The ridge is built in the window's frame, cut to the inner rectangle, then turned. -32768 follows the window's rotation plus its parents', not the tween offset. The window's angle governs the layers it holds (0x5218F).
- 68c3407: `_STRR(l,-99999)` is the follow-the-window sentinel (0x845EB). -100001 is an ordinary angle and wraps to 7999.

Scene 300's windows 28 and 33 now lean -13.870° and +11.525°, and their inner corners match the PS4 trace to 0.1 px. The slices still appear together, not right → middle → left.

An independent review (four lenses, each finding put to a skeptic; `scratch/K53/angle-fix/check`) found the geometry matches the PS4's pieces: the inner piece to 0.01 px, the ridge and feather pieces to under 1 px. Straight windows are unchanged, and the angle survives saves, rollback and motions. Two defects were real, both single pixels on turned edges where pieces met at T-junctions. A held layer showed at full strength outside a soft window's fade (scene 432 steps 1705 / 3883), and picture pixels showed with no ridge over them (scene 742 step 5071).

- 8f87461 builds the mask, its bands and the ridge on one vertex grid.
- b0fcaa0 keeps the straight scissor when the effect passes are off.
- 5337feb reuses `Projection.LayerRot` for -32768. A held layer's own angle turns only its own crop piece, which can only widen the mask (0x4F983).

## 2. Issue #15: portable saves and relative game folders

- `ProfileDirectory` is the one selector. On Windows and Linux an existing **`save`** folder beside the programs wins over the per-user profile; the game, both launchers and crash logging share it. `--save-dir` / `--data` still win. macOS stays in Application Support.
- Both Saves pages offer **Keep saves next to the program (portable)**. The shared switch refuses a running game, an unwritable program folder and overlapping folders. It uses the existing save backup first, recursively copies the profile and compares every copied file byte for byte. A successful switch requires reopening the launcher; settings, folder and save actions and PLAY are guarded until then.
- Review correction 90794e6: both switches disable during an update and after a successful copy until reopening, with command-change notifications and direct-call guards. The Avalonia update guard was missing, and either launcher could previously attempt a second switch against its old in-memory profile.
- Turning off copies back first. Because `save` itself is the marker, the explicit off action then renames it to `save-backup-yyyyMMdd-HHmmss` (with a numeric suffix if needed). Nothing is discarded. This rename and the required reopen are deliberate choices.
- Review correction f0bb6ce: a same-timestamp destination backup is preserved as `...-destination` instead of being overwritten by the source's recursive copy. An absent destination is copied and verified as `...-incomplete-yyyyMMdd-HHmmss` before its final rename, so a failed first copy never activates a partial `save`.
- Claude's review (19ad150): the Windows switch now asks CanExecute again when the game starts and ends, as PLAY does; before, it kept its enabled state from before PLAY (the direct-call guard still refused). Pinned in LauncherModel.cs.
- `GameFolderPath` resolves `--game`, `TSUKI_GAME_DIR` and `game_dir.txt` from `AppContext.BaseDirectory`, never the working directory. Typed relative text is preserved; picker results on the program's drive become relative only in the portable profile (b335279: a per-user `game_dir.txt` is shared by every copy of the port on the PC, so it stays absolute). Rooted paths stay unchanged. The game, CLI, launch plans and both launcher folder pages share the rule.
- Release review of 2026-11-27, with a test that failed first for each:
  - 7d3e29d: the Linux launcher holds `launcher.lock` in the profile with FileShare.None (an exclusive lock) while its window runs, so copying it failed and the switch never worked on Linux or the Steam Deck. The copy now leaves `launcher.lock` and the game's `game.lock` behind.
  - 51f18c3: PLAY, Back up, Import and Restore checked only the game, so a game started or a save restored during the copy landed in the profile being left. Both launchers disable them during the copy, and the guard every profile change asks refuses then.
  - ddd1af9: turning off named the portable folder's own backup, whose path had just moved. It now names the backup of the save the copy replaced.
  - 341b388: `release-check` and `release-sums` refuse any path through a folder named `save` (or `save-backup-...`, `save-incomplete-...`): a release carrying one would become every installing player's profile, and the published v0.1.10 updater has no guard for it.
- Apply, rollback, interrupted-update repair and `.old` cleanup all exclude `save`. Guide section 5.7, both text formats and one README line describe the player-facing rules.

## 4. PS4 versus Switch

The supplied references have slanted slice edges, and the PS4 has them too (2026-11-27, `scratch/K53/angle-check`). The crop pieces 0x4C720 and the ridge 0x4DC60 turn a crop window's clip and ridge by its +0x15C angle (STCH a19 / `_STRR`) about the crop centre, which layers.md had described as unread (erratum there). In scene 300 only the animated windows 28 (-13.87°) and 33 (+11.525°) show it; the static 16 and 21 are turned but at alpha 0. Their seams lie at x 1140.8 / 1274.2 / 1407.5 and 698.2 / 588.1 / 478.0 at y 0 / 540 / 1080, within 2 px and 0.05° of the Switch frames. The port drew them straight at 1289 and 596; it now draws them at 1140.9 / 1274.2 / 1407.6 and 698.1 / 588.0 / 477.9, within 0.13 px of the PS4 (step 4510). Only the right → middle → left order is Switch-only.

The port draws the angle since 1641bac (the owner approved it on 2026-11-27). 446 crop-window settings in 117 scripts carry a nonzero angle, 38 of them with a ridge (16 scenes); the 90° windows of scenes 334, 362 and 370 change most. Only windows with a nonzero angle take the new path; straight windows, the alpha-0 rule and issue #14 draw as before.

## 5. Not run or not proved

- `UploadTimingTests` was never run. No visible game or launcher window, no real Linux / macOS desktop, and no real Mac was used. Linux UI coverage is through the Linux model and headless Avalonia.
- No reference video was downloaded. The owner's supplied console images were used instead. The reporter has not confirmed the fix.
- The alpha-0 rule rests on the eboot and its emulation (and the blend table of compositing.md 4.1), not on a PS4 capture; the 1,260 held layers of `invisible-window-census.txt` were not each checked for a later fade-in. The Switch's right → middle → left order is not explained (knife ignores `_FANX` too) and was not copied.
- The port draws a window, its held layers and its ridge straight over the screen; the PS4 composes them offscreen and lays them at the window's alpha. While a ridge window fades the port is off by up to t(1 - a)(R - d) (t the ridge profile, R its colour, d the backdrop), as v0.1.10 was. The ridge's solid outer half, which the PS4's mask pass lets through (0x4C720's eight clip pieces at the window's alpha), is cut by the port's scissor: light gaps of 2 px between scene 320's panels and 5 px at the screen edge.
- The PS4's composite through the mask pass is not modelled for the ridge beyond the hard inner cut, shocked or not. The re-masks of a held face layer and of the effect-0x42 path keep the window's inset on the PS4; the port re-masks every held picture once. `ClipOf` rounds a moved crop to whole pixels where 0x49A60 keeps the float (up to 0.5 px, also before the shock fix).
- Turned windows (section 4) follow the eboot's geometry; the alpha profile across a turned edge is not proved. The PS4's turned feather pieces are 1 px wider than its straight ones (scale (r + 0.5) / n); the port uses its straight width. A turned ridge window less than four insets wide or high keeps one inner fill, so single-pixel gaps at its edge are still possible there; no retail window is that narrow (the narrowest is 8.6 insets). A turned ridge window is cut hard at its inner rectangle like a straight one, so its outer feather and the ridge's outer half are missing there too. Turned rule windows and `-32768` have no retail use and are pinned only by synthetic tests. The only console frames compared are the owner's three Switch frames.
- The prologue sweep sampled every other logic step; it found no second instance, not proof that none exists. The script census was a lead list, not a playthrough of every affected scene. Full-route console comparisons were not made. Mio's right edge is exactly her script's crop boundary (x = 46 + 740 = 786); no console capture of that moment was supplied, so its artistic match is not verified.
- Program-folder writability and game-running refusals use probes / stand-ins, not a real Program Files install. Updater proof is on synthetic releases and journals and on loopback updates from the published v0.1.10 packages to the v0.1.11 candidates (section 3), not on GitHub. No push, release or GitHub comment was made.
