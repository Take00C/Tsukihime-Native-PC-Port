# K15: route sweep (the whole story played in the real app and looked at)

Package K15, run on 2026-10-26. Trigger: K3 reached all 549 scenes logically, but nobody had looked at most of the game. This package played the Arcueid route, the Ciel route (normal and
extra ending), the 24 bad / dead-end episodes, the Q&A corner, the chapter title cards and the staff credits in the real `Tsuki.App` (hidden window, `--mute`, `SDL_AUDIO_DRIVER=dummy`), took
about 22,000 screenshots, read the contact sheets and fixed what was clearly wrong. Nothing is committed. `artifacts/play*`, `%APPDATA%\Tsuki` and the game folder were not touched
(`--save-dir scratch/K15/...`, builds in `artifacts/K15`); the first hours ran at below-normal priority, the rest at normal priority after the lead's go-ahead.

## 2. How it was done

Tools added (verification only; the game does not use them):

| tool | what |
|---|---|
| `Tsuki.App --sweep-dir DIR` (`SweepRecorder.cs`, `AppArgs.cs`, `GameApp.cs`) | small screenshots (canvas / `--sweep-scale`, default 480 x 270) and `manifest.tsv` (seq, file, step, tag, script, scene id, UI, text). Tags: `scene` (`--sweep-scene N` steps after a scene starts), `choice` (a list opened), `movie` (45 steps after a start, then every 240, three per movie), `end` (a story ended), `m` (`--sweep-messages N`: every N-th message once it is fully revealed and faded in), `pic` (`--sweep-changes GAP`: the committed pictures changed), `t` (`--sweep-every N` steps), `_MISS` suffix (the frame had textures that were not resident). The log gets `scene ID NAME`, `movie N start / end`, `choice-decided ...`, `story ended REASON` lines |
| `--exit-after-ends N`, `--sweep-tour FILE [--tour-cap N] [--tour-end]` | stop after N stories; or jump to each flowchart node of a file in turn (the chart's Jump, `AppServices.FlowchartJump`) and play it to its end |
| script verbs `scene=ID`, `jumpnode=NODE`, `replay=SCENE` (`InputScript.cs`) | start a story scene (the staff roll is 4000), jump to a flowchart node, start a gallery replay, from a script like `20:scene=300` |
| `AutoPlayer` (App) | with a save slot the title's cursor is on Continue: the autoplay now starts the next playthrough itself (`StartScene(NewGameSceneId())`) instead of pressing Enter on Continue |
| `Tsuki.Cli play --list-scenes`, `--explore --explore-dump FILE` | the scene table (index, name, scene id, node, jump targets), and the choice lists that reach every scene (`FILE`) and every scene a playthrough ends in (`FILE.ends`), one playthrough per ` | `-separated part |
| `GameApp.LogState` | the `log` script verb now also prints the page (messages, F823), window alpha, text fade and F1103 |

Contact sheets, coverage and tours are scratch tools in `scratch/K15/tools` (git-ignored): `sheets.py` (labelled sheets of 12..35 thumbnails from a sweep folder), `multi.py`, `pre_end.py`
(the shots before every `end`), `allscenes.py` (one scene-start shot per scene), `covscenes.py` (scenes entered per log), `movies.py` (every chapter-card / ending movie started by script, in
paced mode with real decoding). The sheets are 4 x 3 .. 7 x 5 tiles of 480 x 270 at 0.65..1.0 scale. The first long run alone gave about 300 sheets; I read roughly 130 of them (every shot of the first chapters, then
every 3rd to 6th shot, plus the scene / choice / movie shots of most of both routes), the movie runs, the dead-end tour's pre-`end` frames and the Q&A runs. Coverage of the middle chapters
is therefore a sample, not every frame; every scene has at least one shot on disk.

## 3. Found and fixed

| # | what was wrong (where seen) | cause and fix | proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The last message stayed on screen over every story movie and chapter-card transition**, and over any picture change that hides the text (`04_00_ARC04_4_1` before movie 5, the endings' cards, QA scenes; first seen on the contact sheets of the first long run) | `GameHost.DrawPage` ignored **F1103** ("the text is hidden until the next message": `_CLO2`, `_CLO3`, `_TOMP`, `_FADS`' 4th argument, the prompts). The PS4 HUD draws the current message only while F1103 == 0 (VA 0x54BD8 `cmp F1103, 0; jne` over the block draws at 0x54C2F / 0x54C71 / 0x54CAC) and the earlier ones through a loop that needs it too (VA 0x550F7 / 0x551D9, history loop 0x55930). `DrawPage` now returns while F1103 != 0. **K3's `OpenChoice` set F1103 = 1 for a list**: the eboot does not (the SELD handler 0x9B1DC and the list setup 0xA5F0D write no F1103, `xref 0x164BA6E` shows no writer there; knife sets it for the prompt kinds only), and the question has to stay above the lines, so the write is removed | `PageVisibilityTests` (CLO3 hides at once while the window still fades; a layer change with the hide argument hides until the next message; a list choice leaves F1103 clear and the question and lines are drawn); `NamePlateTests` (K8's expectation "F1103 is 1 while a choice is open" was K3's mistake: it now sets F1103 by hand) |
| 2 | **`@x` messages printed over the end of the previous line** (291 messages; `08_01_CIEL08_1`: the message starting "All right" then the one starting "With my": the sentence started at the left edge on top of the previous one) | nothing set the layout's continue position (`LayoutParams.SavedX`, global VA 0x178BE20). `_ZM` takes it from the message before it on the page: `0xCAD10` gives the x of the last used glyph record, the HUD's history loop and `_ZM` (via 0x7C340, VA 0x7C66C..0x7C683) store `16.5267 + x` (`MessageWindow.ContinueX`, `PageMessage.SavedX`, `LayoutText(..., savedX)`); the first message of a page has none | `AtXContinuationTests` (4), real data `PageLayoutOnRealScenesTests.AnAtXMessageOfCiel08StartsRightOfTheEndOfThePreviousOne` (previous line ends at x 289.3, the @x message starts at 305.9); picture `scratch/K15/o4/cmp.png` |
| 3 | **Speech-bubble windows drew their text at the full-screen origin** (all 25 QA "Teach Me, Miss Ciel" episodes: the text sat at the top left, outside its bubble) | `GameHost.PageOrigins` used (30, 35) / (87, 49) for every window. A bubble (F1105 / F1109 3..55) has its own origin: `MessageWindow.BubbleTextOrigin(window, F1106, F1107)` (VA 0x546CF..0x558B8), now passed to `PagePositions`. The layout call (`MainBoxParams`) already used it, the draw did not | `PageVisibilityTests.ASpeechBubbleWindowDrawsItsTextAtTheBubblesOwnOrigin...`, real data `TheSpeechBubbleOfQa03StartsItsTextAtItsOwnOrigin` (window 49: origin 59 units = 177 px, first glyph at 177 px); picture `scratch/K15/qa5/sheets/q_000004.png` |
| 4 | **Holes in a picture for a frame (20..65 tiles of a CG missing) at the first frame of a scene** while the cache was at its cap (531 `texture not resident` lines in the 5 M-step run, 24 distinct frames) | the look-ahead uploads a scene's tiles ahead of time; they sit at the tail of the LRU order until a frame draws them, and the uploads of the very frame that needs the rest evict them. `TextureCache.Touch(key)` (new, moves a resident key to the front) is called by `TextureFeeder.EnsureResident` for every resident tile the frame draws before the missing ones are queued | `TextureCacheBoundTests.ATouchedTextureIsTheNewestAndSurvivesTheEvictionsThatFollow` (GPU); the same replay run after the fix: 0 missing picture tiles over 153,407 + 68,040 steps |
| 5 | **The app ended with an exception on the first gallery replay started from the title's gallery** (`ArgumentOutOfRangeException` in `UiRoot.Step`, found by running Title > Gallery > Ending List > Yes with the saved profile) | the gallery screen's `OnClosed` hands over to the story with `Root.Clear()`; `UiRoot.Step` was walking the stack from the top and removing finished screens, so with the title below the gallery the loop index ran past the emptied stack. The loop now skips indices past the end. K5b's replay test had no title under the gallery | `FrameworkTests.AScreenThatClearsTheStackWhenItLeavesDoesNotBreakTheStepOverTheScreensBelowIt` (fails without the fix, checked); in the app: replay of QA_12 from the ending list plays, ends and returns to the gallery |

Looked at and found right (no change): the title with Continue (K14) and the gallery hub with the CG grid and ending list (the 3 main cards unlocked after the run, the 21 bad-end plates locked until their episode was played) and the replay dialog; all 27 chapter title cards (`phasetitle_*`, white frame included: it is in the movie files, `ffmpeg` frame check) and
the ending / credit movies; the prologue (`00_01`) with its movie and the scripted `00_02`; the scrolling staff credits at the end of the Arcueid and Ciel routes; the Q&A corner (QACALL hint prompt, the 24 episodes, QA_25); the
choice screens (question above numbered lines, dimmed earlier text, locked lines); the ja, zc, zt text (first chapters).
